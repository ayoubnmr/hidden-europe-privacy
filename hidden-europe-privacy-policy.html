Hidden Europe: Find & Seek

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Hidden Europe: Find & Seek (“the App”) is a mobile hidden-object puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information We Collect

The current version of Hidden Europe: Find & Seek does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers.

The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Local Game Data

The App stores limited game-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Completed scenes
  • Unlocked scenes
  • Game progress
  • App settings

This information remains on your device and is used only to provide game functionality. We do not receive or access this locally stored game data.

Internet and Online Services

The current version of the App operates offline and does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

The current version of the App does not use:

  • Advertising services
  • Analytics services
  • User accounts
  • Cloud storage
  • Remote servers
  • Social login
  • In-app purchases

Children's Privacy

Hidden Europe: Find & Seek is designed primarily as a relaxing puzzle game for a general and adult audience.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

Because the current version of the App does not collect personal information, we do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Data Security

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device using standard application storage mechanisms provided by the Android platform.

Deleting the App may also delete locally stored game progress and settings.

Future Changes

Future versions of the App may introduce additional services, such as advertising.

If the App's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the applicable services, information collected, purposes of processing, and third-party providers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with an updated “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Hidden Europe: Find & Seek, please use the developer contact information provided on the App's Google Play listing.
